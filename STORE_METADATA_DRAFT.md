## Full description

Tiqkets turns your walk-in queue into a smooth, no-app-download experience, for
you and your customers.

No more shouting token numbers. No more customers crowding your counter. With
Tiqkets, customers join your queue and get updates straight on WhatsApp, while
you manage everything from a simple, fast dashboard.

WHY BUSINESSES CHOOSE TIQKETS?

- Customers join instantly via WhatsApp or QR scan, zero app download needed for them
- Real-time queue status keeps your counter calm and organized
- Automatic WhatsApp notifications tell customers exactly when it's their turn
- Works for retail check-ins, dine-in seating, take-away orders, and multi-stage service counters
- Multi-language support so you can serve customers in their preferred language
- Built-in analytics to track wait times, footfall, and service speed

QUEUE MODES FOR EVERY BUSINESS

- FIFO Queue: Simple first-come, first-served ticketing
- Dine-In: Table queue management for restaurants
- Take-Away: Order-ready notifications for quick service
- Check-In: QR-based verification for events and appointments
- Multi-Stage: Track customers across multiple service steps

BUILT FOR YOUR TEAM

- Fast, intuitive Process Queue screen to call, serve, or skip customers
- Admin controls for staff, counters, and settings
- Secure, reliable performance, built to handle high-volume, high-traffic events

Whether you're running a retail store, restaurant, clinic, or a large-scale
event, Tiqkets keeps your line moving and your customers informed, without
making them install another app.

Start your free trial today and see why businesses trust Tiqkets to manage their
queues.

## App access for review

The owner must provide a stable review/demo account that does not expose real
customer data. Supply the username and password directly in each private store
review form, never in Git. Review notes should explain how to reach customer,

## Privacy and policy facts requiring owner confirmation

The client source shows use of account/profile details, optional profile photos,
foreground location, camera QR scanning, notifications, queue and appointment
activity, and event/organization workflows. The final declarations must also
include anything collected or shared by the production API and third-party
services.

Confirm before completing Apple App Privacy or Google Data safety:

- Exact account fields stored by the production service
- Whether precise or approximate location is transmitted or retained
- Whether uploaded profile photos are retained and for how long
- Analytics, crash reporting, advertising, tracking, or device identifiers
- Third parties receiving any user data, including messaging and storage vendors
- Retention and deletion procedures
- Whether users can create accounts in the app and the in-app deletion path
- Encryption in transit and the production data-security contact
- Whether any paid digital features or subscriptions exist
- Intended age group and whether children are part of the target audience
