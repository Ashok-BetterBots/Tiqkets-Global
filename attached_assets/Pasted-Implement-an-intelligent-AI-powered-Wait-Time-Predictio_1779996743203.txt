Implement an intelligent AI-powered Wait Time Prediction feature that becomes a core capability of Tiqkets. Whenever a customer joins a queue, the system should automatically estimate how long they are likely to wait and the approximate time they will be served. The prediction should not be a static calculation; instead, it should continuously learn and improve using historical queue data and actual service outcomes. The prediction engine should consider multiple factors such as industry type, organisation, branch location, queue type, date, day of the week, time of day, customer volume, staff availability, service counters, average service duration, peak hours, holidays, appointments, priority customers, and past queue performance patterns. Over time, the system should compare predicted waiting times with actual waiting times and use those differences to refine future predictions, making the estimates increasingly accurate for each organisation and branch. The feature should work in real time, automatically recalculating wait times whenever customers join or leave the queue, counters open or close, appointments are added, or service conditions change.

The estimated waiting time and expected service time should be clearly displayed within the customer application and communicated through WhatsApp messages and push notifications. For example, when a customer joins a queue, they should receive a message informing them of their queue number, current position, estimated waiting time, and expected service time. If significant changes occur in the queue and the prediction changes meaningfully, the customer should receive an updated notification. To give organisations full control over customer communications, administrators should have settings that allow them to enable or disable wait time predictions, WhatsApp notifications, push notifications, and automatic updates independently. The system should also provide an analytics dashboard where organisations can monitor average waiting times, prediction accuracy, peak periods, service efficiency, and historical trends. The overall solution should be lightweight, scalable, and designed to evolve into a self-learning prediction engine that becomes more accurate as Tiqkets grows and processes more queue data across different industries and locations.
