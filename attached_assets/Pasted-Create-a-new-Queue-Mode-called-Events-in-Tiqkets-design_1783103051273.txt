Create a new **Queue Mode** called **Events** in Tiqkets, designed specifically for managing large-scale events, exhibitions, annual sales, product launches, trade shows, and crowd-controlled entry. This queue mode should provide a complete end-to-end customer journey from event registration to exit while giving organizers complete visibility and control over visitor flow. The Event Organizer should first be able to create and configure an event by entering details such as Event Name, Description, Venue, Event Dates, Operating Hours, Slot Duration, Maximum Capacity per Slot, Registration Fee (advance amount), Terms & Conditions, and upload branding assets including the event banner, logo, promotional images, venue map, and other media. These assets should automatically be used throughout the customer experience, including the registration landing page, digital ticket, admin dashboard, and all WhatsApp communications. Organizers should also be able to customize WhatsApp and email templates using placeholders for customer and event details. The system should integrate with Razorpay for online payments, with all Razorpay credentials (Key ID, Key Secret, Webhook Secret, etc.) securely stored and accessed through environment variables (.env file) and never hardcoded anywhere in the application. Only after Razorpay confirms a successful payment through webhook verification should the booking be confirmed and the ticket generated. Failed, cancelled, or pending payments must not generate tickets, and users should be allowed to retry the payment. Every successful payment should create a payment record with Transaction ID, Payment Status, Timestamp, Amount Paid, and Booking Reference for auditing and reconciliation. Customers should receive a unique registration URL that opens a beautifully designed event landing page displaying the event banner, gallery, event description, venue, dates, timings, registration fee, available slots, FAQs, and terms. Customers register by entering their details, selecting their preferred date and time slot, specifying the number of accompanying family members if applicable, and completing the payment. Upon successful payment, generate a unique Booking ID and a professionally designed QR Code ticket resembling an airline boarding pass containing the event branding, organizer logo, customer details, booking details, QR Code, slot information, Google Maps link, instructions, support contact, and payment confirmation. Immediately send the ticket through WhatsApp and email, followed by automated reminder messages one day before the event, one hour before the slot, and when the customer's slot becomes active. Customers can then arrive at the venue and either scan the QR code or tap an "I'm Here" button from WhatsApp, changing their status to Arrived. Security personnel should use the Tiqkets mobile interface to scan the QR code, validate the booking, prevent duplicate entry, verify slot timing, and check the customer in while automatically updating live occupancy. The customer status should then change to Shopping or Attending Event. If purchases are involved, the billing counter should scan the QR code to automatically retrieve the booking, display the advance payment amount, and allow the operator to redeem it against the final invoice while preventing duplicate redemption. At the exit gate, the QR code should be scanned again to record the exit time, calculate the total visit duration, reduce the live occupancy count, and mark the visit as completed. Immediately after exit, automatically send a personalized thank-you WhatsApp message containing a feedback survey asking customers to rate their overall experience, crowd management, staff behaviour, organization, billing experience (if applicable), and provide suggestions. Every stage of the customer journey—including Registration Started, Registration Completed, Payment Pending, Payment Successful, Ticket Generated, Reminder Sent, Arrived, Checked-In, Shopping/Attending, Billing Completed, Advance Redeemed, Exited, and Feedback Submitted—should be tracked in real time and visible in the admin dashboard with complete audit logs. The application should also include role-based dashboards with KPIs relevant to each role. The Super Admin and Event Organizer dashboards should display Total Registrations, Total Revenue Collected, Successful Payments, Failed Payments, Pending Payments, Slot Utilization Percentage, Current Occupancy, Maximum Capacity, Available Capacity, Expected Arrivals, Expected Exits, Check-ins Today, Average Entry Time, Average Visit Duration, Billing Completed, Advance Redemption Value, No-Shows, Late Arrivals, Feedback Response Rate, Average Customer Rating, Peak Hours, Occupancy Trend Graphs, Registration Trends, Revenue Trends, and Event Progress. Security staff should see only operational metrics such as Current Occupancy, Current Slot, Customers Waiting to Check-In, Customers Already Checked-In, Invalid Scan Attempts, Duplicate Entry Attempts, and Emergency Alerts. Billing staff should see Customers Awaiting Billing, Advance Payments Available for Redemption, Total Redeemed Amount, Pending Redemptions, and Billing Queue. Parking staff should see Parking Occupancy, Available Spaces, Overflow Status, and Expected Incoming Vehicles. Customer Support should see Live Customer Status, Failed Payments, Registration Issues, Pending Assistance Requests, and Recent WhatsApp Conversations if integrated. All dashboards should refresh in real time without manual reloads and provide filters by event, date, slot, and status. The Events queue mode should seamlessly integrate into the existing Tiqkets platform while maintaining the same design language, user experience, role-based permissions, and architecture, ensuring a premium, scalable, secure, and production-ready solution capable of handling thousands of concurrent users and high-volume events.
