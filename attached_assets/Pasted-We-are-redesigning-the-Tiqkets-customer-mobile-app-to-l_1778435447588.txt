We are redesigning the Tiqkets customer mobile app to look modern, lively, premium, consumer-focused, and emotionally engaging similar to apps like Zomato, Uber, Airbnb, and Apple-designed consumer apps.

The current app feels:

Too dark and flat
Too many boxed elements
Low visual hierarchy
Empty spaces without purpose
Functional but not delightful
No emotional engagement
Looks like an internal utility app

We want:

Smooth modern gradients
Soft shadows and glassmorphism
Rich cards
Better spacing and typography
Dynamic homepage
Visual storytelling
Consumer-app feel
Premium SaaS quality
Friendly but professional UI

DO NOT change the backend logic or existing features.
This is purely a UI/UX redesign and component restructuring.
