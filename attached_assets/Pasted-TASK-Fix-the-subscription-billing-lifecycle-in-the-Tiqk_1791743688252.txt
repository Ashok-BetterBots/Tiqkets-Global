Target behaviour (standard SaaS practice):

A. SUBSCRIPTION STATES
   - trialing: in free trial, full access.
   - active: paid, auto-renews every period until the customer cancels.

   - active + cancel_at_period_end = true: customer cancelled; full access until current_period_end, then no renewal.
   - expired: period ended after cancellation, OR grace period ended / Razorpay subscription halted. Access blocked.
   Add fields if missing (additive): cancel_at_period_end (bool), cancelled_at, scheduled_plan_id, scheduled_change_at, grace_until.

B. ACCESS RULE (single helper, used everywhere)
   Create ONE function, e.g. hasActiveSubscription(org), and use it in every place that currently checks status:

            OR (cancel_at_period_end = true AND now <= current_period_end)
   Never block access just because a plan change is scheduled.
