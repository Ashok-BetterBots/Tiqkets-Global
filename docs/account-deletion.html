TIQKETS GLOBAL

Delete your account

You can request deletion of a TIQKETS account and its associated personal data from the app or by email.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Sign in to TIQKETS.
  2. Open Profile or Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm the request.

Request deletion by email

Email hello@tiqkets.in from the address registered to the account. Use the subject “TIQKETS account deletion” and include the account email or registered phone number and whether it is a customer, organization, or event account.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • The account and personal profile information.
  • Authentication credentials and active sessions.
  • Optional profile images and account-linked contact details.

Retention

After identity verification, deletion is completed within 30 days. Records required for fraud prevention, tax, accounting, dispute handling, or other legal duties may be restricted and retained only for the required period. Aggregated or anonymized information that no longer identifies a person may remain in service analytics.

Deleting an organization owner account also removes access for its staff. Export any records you need before submitting the request.
