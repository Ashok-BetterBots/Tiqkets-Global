TIQKETS helps businesses manage queues, tokens, appointments and event check-ins from one dashboard. Customers can join using a QR code or WhatsApp and receive updates without installing the business app.

KEEP YOUR LINE MOVING
• Call, serve, skip and transfer customers with simple queue controls.
• Manage first-come-first-served queues, dine-in seating, takeaway orders and multi-stage services.
• Send WhatsApp updates when it is a customer's turn.

MANAGE EVENTS AND YOUR TEAM
• Scan QR codes to verify tickets and check in attendees.
• Manage staff, counters and organization settings.
• View queue activity, wait times, footfall and service reports.
• Support customers in multiple languages.

Designed for retail stores, restaurants, service counters, clinics and events. An account with a participating TIQKETS organization is required for business management features.
