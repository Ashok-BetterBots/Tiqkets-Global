Tiqkets turns your walk-in queue into a smooth, no-app-download experience for you and your customers.

No more shouting token numbers. No more customers crowding your counter. With Tiqkets, customers join your queue and get updates straight on WhatsApp, while you manage everything from a simple, fast dashboard.

WHY BUSINESSES CHOOSE TIQKETS

• Customers join instantly via WhatsApp or QR scan—zero app download needed for them
• Real-time queue status keeps your counter calm and organized
• Automatic WhatsApp notifications tell customers exactly when it's their turn
• Works for retail check-ins, dine-in seating, takeaway orders, and multi-stage service counters
• Multi-language support helps serve customers in their preferred language
• Built-in analytics track wait times, footfall, and service speed

QUEUE MODES FOR EVERY BUSINESS

• FIFO Queue: Simple first-come, first-served ticketing
• Dine-In: Table queue management for restaurants
• Takeaway: Order-ready notifications for quick service
• Check-In: QR-based verification for events and appointments
• Multi-Stage: Track customers across multiple service steps
