Delete your account

Use this form to request deletion of a TIQKETS account and the personal data associated with it.

What happens next

  • We verify the request using the email address or phone number registered to the account.
  • After verification, the account and its personal profile data are deleted within 30 days.
  • Records that must be kept for fraud prevention, tax, accounting, or other legal duties are restricted and retained only for the required period.
  • Aggregated or anonymized information that can no longer identify you may remain in service analytics.

Deleting an organization owner account also removes access for its staff. Export any records you need before submitting the request.

Enter at least one identifier registered to the account.
